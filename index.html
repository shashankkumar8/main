I'm Shashank Kumar, a Computer Science and Engineering student at ABES Engineering College. I like exploring how software can make complicated workflows feel clearer, calmer and more useful.

Most of my learning happens by building: shaping an idea, tracing the edge cases, making the interface understandable and documenting what the system really does.

I'm especially interested in full-stack engineering, data-rich interfaces, graph systems and human-centred tools.
