I'm Shashank — a Computer Science student who enjoys building things end to end.

My work moves across product interfaces, backend services, data models and the small engineering decisions that make a system dependable. I enjoy taking a messy workflow, finding its underlying structure and turning it into something clear enough to use and explain.

Recently, that has meant building a shared care workspace, a merchant reconciliation workbench, graph-based incident investigations and evidence-led financial analysis. Different domains, one recurring question: how can software make the important parts easier to see?

I'm currently deepening my foundations in software engineering, data structures and algorithms while continuing to ship practical full-stack projects.
